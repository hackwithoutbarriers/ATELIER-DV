Frequently asked questions about the DV Lottery

What is the DV Lottery (Diversity Visa)?

The U.S. government’s annual program makes immigrant visas available through a random draw to people born in countries with historically low immigration to the United States. Entries are submitted only at dvprogram.state.gov.

Who can participate?

You must be born in an eligible country (or qualify through a spouse or parent under chargeability rules) and have a high school education or qualifying work experience in the past 5 years (O*NET). A valid passport is required for the principal applicant starting with DV-2027.

Can a married couple submit two entries?

Yes. Each spouse may submit a separate entry as the principal applicant and list the other spouse and all eligible children. This gives the family two entries, not each person better odds. Each person may submit only one principal entry.

What are the photo requirements?

JPEG, 600 × 600 pixels, no more than 240 KB, taken within 6 months, plain background, no filters or retouching. Atelier DV checks your file on your device; it is never uploaded.

When does registration open?

Dates vary by program year and are announced by the Department of State. Check travel.state.gov. Atelier DV displays official announcements when they are published.

Does Atelier DV submit an entry for me?

No. The tool helps you prepare, check and organize your information. You submit your entry yourself at dvprogram.state.gov. Atelier DV is not affiliated with or endorsed by the U.S. government.
