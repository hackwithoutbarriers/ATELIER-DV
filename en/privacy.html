Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026.

1. Publisher

Atelier DV is published by Remy A. (Cybersecurity Analyst & Developer). Contact: agbokpeablamvi@gmail.com.

2. Your entry information stays with you

Everything you enter (identity, family, address, passport, etc.) is stored only in your browser’s local storage. It is not sent to our servers, and we cannot access it. Photos are analyzed on your device (format, dimensions and file size): the file is neither uploaded nor retained; only its technical characteristics are stored. The “Reset” button or clearing this site’s data in your browser erases everything.

3. Exports, backups and sharing

Exports (Excel, CSV, PDF and JSON) are generated on your device. Excel export loads the SheetJS library from the cdnjs (Cloudflare) CDN, which exposes your IP address to that service. These files contain personal information; protect them. Sharing buttons send only the tool’s link, never your entry information; they open the relevant third-party services (WhatsApp, Telegram, etc.), which are governed by their own policies.

4. Analytics

If enabled, analytics (Google Analytics 4 or cookie-free Plausible) is loaded only after your consent for Google Analytics. Only anonymous events are collected (tab viewed, export, share and language switch), including the active language; never the contents of your entry information, with IP anonymization. You can change your choice at any time using the “Analytics” link at the bottom of the page.

5. Hosting and technical logs

The hosting provider may record technical logs (IP address, date and pages requested) to ensure the service’s security and operation.

6. Your rights

Under Togolese Law No. 2019-014 of October 29, 2019 on the protection of personal data and, where applicable, the GDPR, you have rights of access, rectification, erasure and objection. Because the publisher does not hold your entry information, you exercise these rights directly on your device; for analytics data, contact us. You may contact the data-protection authority in your country.

7. Security

The site is served over HTTPS with security headers. Preferably use a personal device and do not share exported files.
